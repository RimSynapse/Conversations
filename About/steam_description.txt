[h1]RimSynapse - Conversations[/h1]
[b][url=https://github.com/RimSynapse/Conversations/wiki]Official Wiki and Documentation[/url][/b]
[b]Dynamic, memory-aware, and trait-influenced text conversations between your colonists using Large Language Models.[/b]

Bring the social life of your colony to life! RimSynapse Conversations intercepts pawn interactions and translates them into actual written dialogue. What they say is directly shaped by their personal histories, traits, relationships, and current traumas.

---

[h2]Key Features[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]Dynamic Dialogue Generation:[/b] Read actual conversations between pawns as they socialize, work, or fight.
[*] [b]Psychological Integration:[/b] Conversing pawns will naturally bring up their core memories, trust levels, and traumas in their chats. (Requires RimSynapse - Psychology)
[*] [b]Expansion Dialects:[/b] Support for unique dialogue flavors, including psycast interjections, religious debates, and paranoid void whispers.
[*] [b]UI Log Viewer:[/b] Browse recent chatter and browse conversation histories for each pawn.
[/list]

---

[h2]Roadmap[/h2]
Conversations tracks its own release milestones. What ships next is scoped to dialogue features in this mod - other RimSynapse mods keep their own roadmaps.
[list]
[*] [b]0.9 - Group Conversations:[/b] three-way and small-group dialogue around meal halls, campfires and huddled shelters, with turn-taking and reactions from listeners in earshot.
[*] [b]0.10 - Expansion Dialects:[/b] activate the DLC-specific flavours listed in the compatibility matrix - noble tone demands and psycast interjections (Royalty), faith debates and conversion attempts (Ideology), xenotype dialects and mechanitor comm chatter (Biotech), void whispers (Anomaly).
[*] [b]Backlog:[/b] player-to-pawn direct dialogue, more Universe Actions (relationship shifts, inspire-to-job), and additional environmental / event triggers.
[/list]
These ship when they are ready - no dates.

[h2]Changelog (v0.8.0) - Event-Driven Talk, Multi-Line Delivery and Voice[/h2]
[list]
[*] NEW - Event-driven conversations: chit-chat and deep-talk topics are now driven by what's actually happening in your colony, with recent events weighted high and conversations pre-staged around notable moments.
[*] NEW - Single-call multi-line conversations: a whole exchange is generated at once and drip-fed line by line to pawns in range, instead of one-shot back-and-forths.
[*] NEW - Conversations now honour the Psychology-authored pawn voice, so each colonist sounds like themselves.
[*] NEW - Two new agent tools: get_chat_history and get_colonist_interests.
[*] Changed: Universe Actions now flag mutating actions, so autonomous runs respect the mutation gate.
[*] Fixed: a no-op Universe Action mood booster now actually applies.
[*] Performance: de-batched the per-tick environmental scan and cached reflection lookups.
[*] Requires Core v0.8.0.
[/list]

[h2]Changelog (v0.7.1) - Deeper, more personal conversations[/h2]
[list]
[*] NEW - 27 conversation topics (up from 6): small talk about weather, food, chores, animals and gossip, and deep talks about grief, regrets, beliefs, trauma, love, mortality and moral lines.
[*] NEW - Colonists talk about their actual surroundings - the room they sleep in and the clothes they wear become conversation topics.
[*] Conversations now draw on real memories: light recent events for small talk, long-standing burdens and specific losses for heart-to-hearts. Deep talks are richer and more personal while small talk stays brief.
[*] Colonists no longer repeat the same topic back-to-back.
[*] Experimental: optional background pre-seeding of conversations for instant delivery (off by default - it can serve stale context).
[*] Requires Core v0.7.1.
[/list]
