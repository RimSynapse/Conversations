[h1]RimSynapse - Conversations[/h1]
[b]📚 [url=https://github.com/RimSynapse/Conversations/wiki]Official Wiki and Documentation[/url][/b]

[b]Companion Mod for RimSynapse.[/b]
Allows colonists to converse dynamically using LLMs.

[h2]Changelog[/h2]
[b]v0.4.0[/b]
- Updated to support RimSynapse Core v0.4.0 (Multi-provider routing and Image generation).

[h2]Compatibility Matrix[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]Royalty DLC:[/b] Compatible and Optional. Enables noble tone demands and psycast interjections.
[*] [b]Ideology DLC:[/b] Compatible and Optional. Enables faith debates and active proselytizing.
[*] [b]Biotech DLC:[/b] Compatible and Optional. Enables xenotype dialects and mechanitor comms.
[*] [b]Anomaly DLC:[/b] Compatible and Optional. Enables paranoid void whispers.
[*] [b]RimSynapse - Psychology:[/b] Compatible and Optional. Colonists will naturally bring up their deep core memories, traumas, and trust levels during everyday conversations.
[/list]

[h2]Requirements[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]RimSynapse - Core[/b] (Must be loaded before this mod)
[/list]
