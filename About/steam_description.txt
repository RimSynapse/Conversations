[h1]RimSynapse - Conversations[/h1]
[b]Dynamic, memory-aware, and trait-influenced text conversations between your colonists using Large Language Models.[/b]

Bring the social life of your colony to life! RimSynapse Conversations intercepts pawn interactions and translates them into actual written dialogue. What they say is directly shaped by their personal histories, traits, relationships, and current traumas.

---

[h2]Key Features[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]Dynamic Dialogue Generation:[/b] Read actual conversations between pawns as they socialize, work, or fight.
[*] [b]Psychological Integration:[/b] Conversing pawns will naturally bring up their core memories, trust levels, and traumas in their chats. (Requires RimSynapse - Psychology)
[*] [b]Expansion Dialects:[/b] Support for unique dialogue flavors, including psycast interjections, religious debates, and paranoid void whispers.
[*] [b]UI Log Viewer:[/b] Browse recent chatter and browse conversation histories for each pawn.
[/list]
