[h1]RimSynapse - Conversations[/h1]
[b][url=https://github.com/RimSynapse/Conversations/wiki]Official Wiki and Documentation[/url][/b]
[b]Dynamic, memory-aware, and trait-influenced text conversations between your colonists using Large Language Models.[/b]

Bring the social life of your colony to life! RimSynapse Conversations intercepts pawn interactions and translates them into actual written dialogue. What they say is directly shaped by their personal histories, traits, relationships, and current traumas.

---

[h2]Key Features[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]Dynamic Dialogue Generation:[/b] Read actual conversations between pawns as they socialize, work, or fight.
[*] [b]Grounded in real events:[/b] An in-code agent picks a concrete beat - a real event, chore or need - before the model writes it, so colonists talk about actual specifics instead of generic filler.
[*] [b]Warden conversations:[/b] Recruiting, converting, enslaving and suppressing prisoners play out as spoken exchanges grounded in the prisoner's real situation. Flavour only - it never changes the vanilla roll.
[*] [b]Psychological Integration:[/b] Conversing pawns will naturally bring up their core memories, trust levels, and traumas in their chats. (Requires RimSynapse - Psychology)
[*] [b]Runs at your pace:[/b] When the AI can't keep up, conversations shed gracefully instead of piling up a backlog - relationships still evolve.
[*] [b]UI Log Viewer:[/b] Browse recent chatter and conversation histories for each pawn.
[/list]

[h2]Roadmap[/h2]
Conversations tracks its own release milestones. What ships next is scoped to dialogue features in this mod - other RimSynapse mods keep their own roadmaps.

[h2]Changelog (v0.9.1) - Linked Memories[/h2]
[list]
[*] Fixed: chit-chat and overheard-conversation memories now record who they are about through Core 0.9's canonical memory linkage, so a conversation about a colonist truly consolidates with later events about them (their death, most importantly). Previously these memories were invisible to relational consolidation.
[*] Requires RimSynapse Core v0.9.0.
[/list]

[h2]Changelog (v0.9.0) - Factions and Living Societies[/h2]
[list]
[*] NEW - Warden conversations: recruiting, converting, enslaving and suppressing a prisoner now play out as spoken exchanges grounded in that prisoner's real situation - their resistance, will, comfort, how they were captured, and (with Ideology) the belief clash. Persuasion can build a little rapport; coercion never does. Flavour only - it never changes the vanilla recruitment or conversion roll.
[*] IMPROVED - Dialogue quality overhaul: before the model writes a line, an in-code agent picks one concrete beat - a real event, chore or need, and each speaker's angle on it - so colonists talk about actual specifics instead of interchangeable filler. Trust, familiarity and opinion shifts are computed in code for consistency.
[*] NEW - Load-adaptive conversations: when the AI can't keep up (a big colony, a slower model, or high game speed), conversations quietly stop generating instead of piling up a backlog - relationships still evolve, you just don't pay for dialogue you can't watch. Speech bubbles still appear at normal speed.
[*] CHANGED - The player-to-storyteller chat window has moved to RimSynapse Core. Conversations now owns pawn-to-pawn dialogue only.
[*] Requires Core v0.9.0; saves and settings carry over unchanged.
[/list]
